# Content licence

Everything in this directory – story text and its structure, translations, shortened
texts, images, audio and fonts – is derived from the companion app of
**My Father's Work**, © Renegade Game Studios, released to the community in June 2026.

Renegade released these files to the community as open resources, without naming a
specific licence. The fan-made companion app
repository (https://github.com/Deusald/MyFathersWork-FanMadeCompanionApp), from which the
story scripts are taken, publishes them under **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial
4.0 International (CC BY-NC 4.0)** – https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/ – and
Chronicle follows the same terms: non-commercial use, with attribution.

**Changes made:** the original Cradle/Twine story scripts were converted into a JSON
data format; text was split into per-language string tables and classified as story,
rules or app instructions; a few errors of the original were fixed; images were cropped
and converted to WebP, audio to MP3. Shortened or simplified texts (if present, marked
`short`) are adaptations written for this project.

**Fan-made scenarios:** `the-thinning-veil/` (_Il Velo Sottile_ / _The Thinning Veil_) is not
from the original. It is a new story written for this project, built on the game's rules,
world and components, and so a derivative work: it is released under the same terms
(CC BY-NC 4.0). The app marks it as unofficial.

This is an unofficial fan project and is not endorsed by Renegade Game Studios.
It may not be used commercially.
