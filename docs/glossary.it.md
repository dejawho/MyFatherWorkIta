# Italian glossary

Terms for `strings.it.json`. There is no official Italian edition: the cards, tiles, boards and
the Village Chronicle are printed in English, so names of printed components stay in English.

## General rules

- **Printed component names stay English**: Town locations (Town Hall, Blacksmith, Church, …),
  Estate Upgrade and Vanity tile names (Storage Shed, …), Experiment titles (Time Machine, …),
  scenario tokens and cards (Hunt, Paradox, Weapon, Separatist, …).
- **Story names in the narrative are translated** (factions, institutions, diseases), unless the
  text means the printed component (a "Separatist token" stays `token Separatist`).
- **Address**: `voi` when the text speaks to the whole table, `tu` for one player (secrets, "your
  Estate", hand-overs).
- Markup stays exactly as it is: `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `{icon:NAME}`, `{0}`, `\` escapes, line
  breaks.
